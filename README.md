TrendKart Fashion Retail Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes TrendKart's fashion retail transaction data to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product trends, store performance, and key business drivers. The dataset was cleaned, validated, and analyzed using Microsoft Excel to generate meaningful business insights.

Problem Statement

TrendKart wants to analyze its retail sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing products and categories, customer purchasing patterns, and revenue drivers across different channels and regions. The analysis aims to support data-driven business decisions and improve overall retail performance.

Dataset Information

- Dataset Name: TrendKart Fashion Enterprise Dataset
- Industry: Fashion Retail
- Business Type: Premium Omni-Channel Fashion Retail
- Data Type: Retail Sales & Transaction Data
- Key Data Areas: Product details, customer information, sales transactions, stores, categories, pricing, discounts, payment methods, and sales channels.

Data Cleaning Process

The following data quality checks and cleaning activities were performed:

1. Checked for missing, blank, and NULL values.
2. Identified duplicate records and duplicate IDs.
3. Checked for inconsistent text formatting.
4. Standardized categorical values where appropriate.
5. Verified numerical and date fields for consistency.
6. Checked data accuracy and structural consistency.
7. Preserved valid records and avoided unnecessary data deletion.
8. Flagged uncertain values for further review instead of making assumptions.

Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-performing products and categories that contribute significantly to revenue.
- Sales variations across different time periods.
- Differences in performance between regions and sales channels.
- Customer purchasing patterns across different customer segments.
- Stores and channels that generate stronger sales performance.
- Products and categories that may require additional promotional attention.

Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Focus inventory and marketing efforts on high-performing products and categories.
- Improve stock planning based on product demand and sales trends.
- Develop targeted promotions for underperforming products.
- Identify high-value customer segments and create targeted offers.
- Monitor online and offline channel performance to optimize sales strategies.
- Compare regional and store-level performance regularly to identify growth opportunities.

Conclusion

The TrendKart Fashion Retail Analysis transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel. The analysis provides a clear view of sales, product, customer, store, regional, and channel performance, helping management make informed and data-driven business decisions.
